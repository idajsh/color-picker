# Color Picker

A tiny browser-based eyedropper tool. Paste a screenshot, drop, or upload an
image, then hover to inspect and click to lock a color. Shows hex, RGB, and HSL,
keeps a row of recent swatches, and copies to your clipboard.

**Live tool: https://idajsh.github.io/color-picker/**

## Usage

- **Paste** a screenshot with `⌘V` (or `Ctrl+V`)
- **Drop** an image onto the panel, or click to browse
- **Hover** to inspect pixels with a zoomed loupe
- **Click** to lock a color and read its values
- **Copy hex** with the button, or click any recent swatch to copy it

## Privacy

Everything runs locally in your browser. Images you load are never uploaded —
they stay in the browser tab and are gone when you close it.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step, no dependencies.
